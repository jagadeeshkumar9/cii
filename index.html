Settings

MQTT connectivity & billing configuration

MQTT Broker Connection

Connect to your real broker over WebSocket — devices publish to device/<DEVICE_ID>/telemetry (e.g. device/TANK-001/telemetry), matching each ESP32's firmware exactly. Until connected, the dashboard runs on simulated live data.

Your ESP32 sketches publish over plain TCP (port 1883) — the broker needs a separate WebSocket listener for this browser to connect. If you're on self-hosted Mosquitto, add a listener 8083 (or 8884 for TLS) websockets block to mosquitto.conf; each device still connects on 1883/8883 unchanged.
